In classical statistics, how we treat prediction depends on strong parametric assumptions. With the incredible growth of AI, where the model is effectively a black box, distribution-free inference is becoming a substantial field in statistics. And it is not only about inference: controlling the risk of predictions and detecting out-of-distribution (OOD) inputs are critical to controlling the quality of these machines.

Here, I introduce the most generic and simple framework among them, **conformal prediction**, and review its representative papers. The primary sources I reference in this section are listed below.
